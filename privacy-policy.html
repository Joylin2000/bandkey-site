Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-09-14

中文

概览：Band Key（"本应用"）是一款本地运行的音乐工具，旨在尽可能不收集任何个人信息。

1. 我们不收集任何数据
Band Key 是一个纯离线的单机应用：没有账号系统、没有后端服务器、没有广告、没有第三方分析 SDK。（可选高级订阅的支付由 Apple 处理，详见第 8 节。）你在应用中创建的一切内容（收藏的和弦、进行、设置等）都只保存在你自己的设备本地。

2. 局域网同步
「乐队模式」仅在用户主动发起时，通过同一局域网进行设备到设备的点对点调号同步。连接过程中仅在设备间传递调号等演奏状态，用于达成同步目的，不会上传到任何服务器，也不会被本应用留存。

3. 网络与权限
本应用不请求相册、通讯录、定位等隐私权限。应用可能会在乐队模式下请求访问本地网络的权限，仅用于点对点同步，不会借此采集数据。

4. 第三方服务
本应用不集成任何第三方内容服务、统计或广告 SDK。

5. 数据处理
由于我们不收集任何用户数据，因此不存在数据存储、传输、出售或共享行为。

6. 儿童隐私
本应用适用于所有年龄用户，且不收集任何用户数据，符合儿童隐私相关要求。

7. 政策变更
如未来本政策发生重大变更，我们会在应用内更新本页面。可随时通过下方联系方式咨询。

8. 订阅与自动续费
「Band Key 高级订阅」为自动续期订阅，由 Apple 通过 App Store 提供与计费。购买即视为同意《Band Key 订阅条款》： • 周期与价格：以购买时 App 内展示为准（月度 / 年度订阅，或一次性终身买断）。
• 自动续订：除非在当前订阅期结束前至少 24 小时在「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」中关闭自动续订，否则订阅将自动续订并从你的 App Store 账户扣款。
• 免费试用（如有）：试用期结束前取消可避免扣费，取消在试用期结束时生效。
• 取消与退款：取消后仍可使用至当前周期结束；退款请通过 Apple 支持（support.apple.com）申请。
• 隐私：上述购买信息仅由 Apple 处理，本应用不收集、不存储、不共享任何支付数据。

English

Overview: Band Key (the "App") is a locally-run music tool designed to collect as little personal information as possible.

1. We collect no data
The App is a fully offline, standalone application: no accounts, no backend servers, no ads, and no third-party analytics SDKs (any optional Premium subscription is billed by Apple — see Section 8). Everything you create in the app (favorites, progressions, settings) stays only on your device.

2. Local network sync
“Band Mode” provides peer-to-peer key-signature sync between devices over the same local network, only when you explicitly initiate it. Only playing context (such as the current key) is exchanged between devices for the purpose of sync; it is not uploaded to any server and is not retained by the App.

3. Network & permissions
The App does not request privacy-sensitive permissions such as photos, contacts, or location. It may request local-network access in Band Mode, used solely for peer-to-peer sync and not for data collection.

4. Third-party services
The App integrates no third-party content, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

5. Data handling
Because the App collects no data, there is no storage, transmission, sale, or sharing of user data.

6. Children’s privacy
The App is suitable for all ages and collects no user data, consistent with children’s-privacy requirements.

7. Policy changes
Should this policy materially change, we will update this page. You can reach us at any time via the contacts below.

8. Subscription & auto-renewal
“Band Key Premium” is an auto-renewable subscription billed by Apple via the App Store. By purchasing you agree to the Band Key Subscription Terms: • Term & price: as shown at the time of purchase (monthly / yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock).
• Auto-renewal: unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions), the subscription renews automatically and your App Store account is charged.
• Free trial (if offered): cancel before the trial ends to avoid being charged; cancellation takes effect at the end of the trial.
• Cancellation & refunds: after cancelling you keep access until the current period ends; refunds are handled via Apple Support (support.apple.com).
• Privacy: purchase data is handled solely by Apple; the App does not collect, store, or share any payment data.
