产品事实（供文案引用）

核心功能
  • 单机和弦词典：反查和弦、音名、级数、简谱对照
  • 演奏浮窗：悬浮琴键视窗，实时跟随当前和弦与下一行，支持「下一行」翻页
  • 调号移调：一键把整首曲目/音阶移到任意调（12 调），级数/简谱同步
  • 乐队模式：局域网（蓝牙/LAN）点对点同步调号，多台设备跟同一个调
  • 乐器切换：钢琴 / 吉他 / 贝斯指板视图
  • 收藏与进行：收藏常用和弦、快速调用常用进行
数据与合规
纯本地单机 App，无账号、无后端服务器、不收集任何用户数据、无网络上传。全部数据存于本机。

描述 Description · English
Band Key is a pocket tool for chord players: look up chords, see the piano keys, transpose on the fly, and keep your whole band in one key.

• Chord lookup: type the notes to identify the chord, with degrees, note names and numbered notation
• Floating performer keys: a floating piano follows the current chord, highlights chord tones in green, and previews next line with one-tap page turn
• 12-key transpose: switch to any key (C, G, D, A, E, F, Bb…) and degrees + notation update together
• Band mode: peer-to-peer key sync over LAN across multiple devices, so guitar, bass, keys and drums stay in the same key
• Instrument views: piano keyboard, guitar and bass fingerboard
• Favorites & progressions: save chords and common progressions
• Premium (optional): unlock all 12 keys & custom modes, unlimited Band Mode, watermark-free export

Runs fully offline: no account, no backend, no data collection. Your practice stays on your device. Great for guitarists, bassists, keyboardists, drummers and pop-music learners.
