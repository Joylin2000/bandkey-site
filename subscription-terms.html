1. 订阅说明
「Band Key 高级订阅」（Premium）为 自动续期订阅（Auto-Renewable Subscription），由 Apple 通过 App Store 提供并计费。订阅用于解锁高级功能：全部 12 个调号与自定义调式、乐队模式无限时长、无水印导出、无限制收藏等。Band Key 提供月度、年度订阅，以及一次性「终身买断」（非消耗型购买）。

2. 价格与周期
订阅价格与周期以购买时 App 内展示为准（当前建议：月度 ¥18 / 月、年度 ¥98 / 年、终身买断 ¥198 一次性）。苹果 App Store 有权根据地区汇率调整显示价格；如价格变更，已生效订阅不受影响，续订价格会提前通过 App Store 提示。

3. 自动续订
除非在当前订阅期结束前至少 24 小时关闭自动续订，否则订阅将按所选周期自动续订，并通过你的 App Store 账户（iTunes 账户）扣款。续订费用在当前周期结束前 24 小时内收取。

4. 管理订阅
你可随时在 iOS「设置」→ 你的 Apple ID →「订阅」 中查看、更改或取消自动续订。取消后，当前订阅周期内仍可继续使用高级功能，直至本期结束，之后不会自动续订。

5. 免费试用（如有）
如提供免费试用（例如年度订阅 7 天试用），试用期结束前取消可避免扣费；试用期结束将自动转为付费订阅并扣款，除非你已提前取消。

6. 退款

7. 隐私
订阅购买与支付信息仅由 Apple 处理，Band Key 不收集、不存储、不共享任何支付数据。详见《Band Key 隐私政策》。

English

1. Subscription overview
“Band Key Premium” is an auto-renewable subscription offered and billed by Apple through the App Store. It unlocks Premium features: all 12 keys and custom modes, unlimited Band Mode, watermark-free export, and unlimited favorites. Band Key offers monthly and yearly subscriptions, plus a one-time “Lifetime” unlock (non-consumable purchase).

2. Price & term
The subscription price and term are as shown in the app at the time of purchase (current suggestion: ¥18/month, ¥98/year, ¥198 lifetime one-time). Apple may adjust displayed prices by region; existing subscriptions are not affected, and renewal prices are shown in advance via the App Store.

3. Auto-renewal
Unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, the subscription renews automatically and your App Store (iTunes) account is charged. Renewal is charged within 24 hours before the end of the current period.

4. Managing subscriptions
You can view, change or cancel auto-renewal at any time in iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions. After cancelling, you keep Premium access until the end of the current period; it will not renew afterwards.

5. Free trial (if offered)
If a free trial is offered (e.g. 7 days for the yearly plan), cancelling before the trial ends avoids being charged. At the end of the trial, the subscription converts to paid and your account is charged unless you cancelled in advance.

6. Refunds
Refunds are handled by Apple. Please submit requests via Apple Support – Request a refund. Band Key does not process payment refunds directly.

7. Privacy
Purchase and payment data is handled solely by Apple; Band Key does not collect, store or share any payment data. See the Band Key Privacy Policy for details.
